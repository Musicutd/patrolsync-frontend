Response Cache

Short-lived caching reduces repeated PostgreSQL work for private platform-console reads. Guard, subscriber, attendance, dispatch, patrol, and safety APIs remain live and uncached.

Safety Rules

Automatic invalidation is enabled.

Successful platform changes clear cached responses. Entries also expire after 5–60 seconds depending on the endpoint. Authentication is checked before every cache hit.

Use ?fresh=1 on a cached GET endpoint to bypass its cache during diagnostics.
