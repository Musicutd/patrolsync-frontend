Response Cache

Reduce repeated PostgreSQL work for private platform-console reads while operational APIs remain live and uncached.

Safety Rules

Automatic invalidation is enabled.

Successful platform changes clear cached responses. Entries expire after 5–60 seconds depending on the endpoint, and authentication is checked before every cache hit.

Use ?fresh=1 on a cached GET endpoint to bypass the cache during diagnostics.
